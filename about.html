Our Story

Urban Leather is a Khulna-based brand of premium genuine leather goods. Every piece starts from real leather — with handcrafted stitching and a premium finish — made to be carried every day and kept for years.

The Craft

Leather, the Way
It Should Feel

Real leather has a grain you can feel and a scent you cannot fake. Every Urban Leather piece starts from that honest material — cut, edged and stitched so the seams hold as long as the hide itself.

With use, genuine leather softens, deepens in colour and takes on a patina that is uniquely yours. That is the difference between an accessory and a companion.

  • Full, natural grain — no synthetic shine
  • Edges burnished and seams stitched tight
  • Hardware and folds built for daily use
